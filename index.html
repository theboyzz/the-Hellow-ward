Hollow Ward

A first-person horror game in a maze that rebuilds itself every run. Find three fuses, restart the service lift, and keep the Warden in your light.

Fuses
Lamp

Move W A S D · Turn arrow keys or mouse · Lamp F or Space

The premise

You are the night electrician at St. Aldric's. The power failed on the lower ward and the only way out is the service lift. It needs three fuses, and they are scattered through corridors that nobody has mapped since the ward closed.

Each run builds a new maze. You see only what your lamp touches, and you remember the rooms you have walked through.

The Warden

It only walks when it is unseen. Hold your beam on it and it stands still, shuddering. The beam costs battery, and the Warden is never far.

  • Lit and in your cone: frozen.
  • Out of sight or in the dark: it hunts you along the corridors.
  • Lamp off or dead: it moves faster than you can run.
  • Each fuse you take makes it quicker.

Controls

  • W A S D move and sidestep
  • Left / Right turn, or move the mouse after you click the view
  • F or Space lamp on and off
  • M sound on and off
  • On a phone, use the buttons under the view.

Tuning

Everything about the pacing lives in one CFG object at the top of the script.

drain
Battery used per second while the lamp is on.
batteryGain
Charge restored by each battery cell.
wardenBase, wardenPerFuse
The Warden's walking speed, and how much faster it gets per fuse.
darkBoost
Speed multiplier when you are in the dark.
grace
Seconds before the Warden starts to move.
coneHalf
How wide the beam counts as "looking at it", in radians.
